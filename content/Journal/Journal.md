# Isadora

I heard some rumbling downstairs. Went to investigate the noise, assuming a thief was on the prowl. To my surprise, the only thing missing was food from the kitchen.

Not the thief I had expected, but one most welcome, as I truly wasn't in the mood for a confrontation. Our food thief must have felt the same, as she was halfway down the street by the time I stepped outside. Of course, it was Isadora. Who else could it be?

Against my better judgement, I began walking after her. No chance I would catch up, thought to myself. Strangely, she seemed to have stopped in her tracks, finally within reach of my voice. She was on her way to the gates, wanting to leave the city, yet didn't know the way. Curious.

What kind of a host would I be if I didn't accompany such a welcome guest on her departure. So, I walked alongside her. Well, in front. She kept her distance. A strange one, though this city seems to produce them in abundance. I asked why. Could not give me a straight answer, only that she must leave. **Her hatred for this place is insurmountable.**

No surprise there. I admire her for it, truly. If I were a little different, perhaps I would have asked to join. It is a shame I didn't recognize the sentiment sooner. Perhaps I could have served as an anchor, someone to relate to. Still, we all do what we must. For her, that lies outside the walls. For me, **it remains here.**

In anticipation, I grabbed a wind up music box on my way out to give as a parting gift. Had her eyes on it since the moment she first stepped inside The Risen Phoenix. Tried convincing her to stay, but even I do not have the strength to stand in the way of **such a decision**. It should remind her of me. I hope she remembers. **She is one of the few.**

**I truly hope our paths cross once more.**

# The Flickering
### 14th of Trudari, 2173

Not how I imagined it, yet in the end I must **thank you, Dev**. Without you dragging me out of that room, I likely would have never stepped **outside the walls**. Only if things didn't escalate so quick. Mirielle is deceivingly capable, I know you two are safe. Yet, I find myself worrying out here, surrounded by darkness. Who knew being deprived of sight would let my thoughts run so freely? It carries an eerie resemblance to my room, I must admit.

**Awan is alive**. Not that I did any better in reaching out, but for two whole years, I haven't seen him. Not knowing where to look was a lie, just to keep my own comfort. Or perhaps to avoid reminding myself of my own mortality. None the less, it fills me with warmth to know he is fine. Well, fine might be an exaggeration. Something changed, and **for the worse**. There is **less life behind those eyes** when he thinks people aren't looking. Must be something to do with the quite one, **Q** his name was I believe.

A pale, blue hue. By the time I realized I was already ravaged by frostbites. A foolish endeavor, yet one **I shall take again** without second thought. That Keeper called Pao acted strange. Wasn't as panicked as one should've been when their dear sun is malfunctioning. I must remember to **keep an eye on him**.

There are two others who joined us on this mission. Tirzah and Nameless. Strange people, secretive, quite. They remind me of the Fade, back when I was very little. Nowadays I'm told to keep my mouth shut, to have been any other way is an amusing thought. Tirzah, the one who totally and without any question is a Keeper, reminded me of something that you two taught me. I shall keep on **smiling and laughing**, that **they can't take away** from us.
